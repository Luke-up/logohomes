At Logo Homes, we offer you a highly specialised and unique timber frame building service that incorporates both the design and the construction of your perfect home.

With us, you can enjoy a bespoke home design, artfully created by taking your needs, wishes and dreams into account. One that not only reflects your personal style but incorporates an intelligent flow for living comfortably whilst blending beautifully into your chosen environment.

The South African Institute of Timber Builders (SAITB) (formerly known as the Timber Frame Builders Association) has awarded Logo Homes with Gold and Silver awards every year they have had them since we first entered in 1998.The awards have been for both the design and construction of our timber frame homes. In fact, with 45 gold and 33 silver awards, we have won the most awards in the history of the awards.

So, whether your aspiration is for a classic Cape Cod style timber framed home featuring shiplap or fibre cement cladding or an open plan light and airy beach house , whether your preference is a pitched roof Victorian style wood home, a rustic log cabin or possibly a more contemporary style home featuring a modern mix of materials, our 27-year history and over 385 finished timber framed homes will ensure that your building experience is both a rewarding and exciting one. There is the added advantage that they are all eco friendly with fantastic insulation.
